\subsection{General Objective}
To examine whether multiomics can be used to characterise the biological signals associated with subtype discrimination in lung squamous cell carcinoma using interpretable machine learning.

\subsection{Specific Objectives}

\begin{itemize}
    \item To characterise clinical characteristics, immune microenvironment, and somatic mutation patterns associated with established \gls{lusc} subtypes.
    \item To compare gene expression, \gls{dna} methylation, \gls{mirna} expression, copy number variation, and RPPA as molecular representations of established \gls{lusc} subtypes.
    \item To evaluate whether different classifier families show consistent subtype-discriminative performance across omics modalities.
    \item To identify molecular features associated with subtype discrimination in each omics modality. %identified by SHAP analysis
    \item To examine whether subtype-discriminative molecular features correspond to subtype-associated patterns of somatic mutation in \gls{lusc}.

\end{itemize}
